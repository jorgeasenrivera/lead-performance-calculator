2 · The four cards

Which card, and why

  • Both quiet. Neither lane is urgent: both small, floor on top, phone under it.
  • Floor hot. You are up, the desk wants you, or a manager is being asked for: the floor lane big with buttons, the phone lane a one-line strip.
  • Phone hot. A desk is being offered to you, or you are next for one: the phone lane big with buttons, the floor lane a strip.
  • One lane. The store runs one room, or you are only on one line: that lane alone, big.

Hot is decided by the server (hotOf in api/_live-standing.mjs), in this order: a desk offer, then up or nudged or asked on the floor, then next on the phone cord. The card never re-decides.

D1 · You're up keeps the rail and gives up its caption

As merged in #382. The rail with its light funnelling in to you is the picture; the caption ("Table 3", where there is one) is what goes. The card comes to about 159 with the strip under it.

Decided 18 September: Keep.

D2 · A desk offer shows the desk row, never the cord

As merged in #377. "Desk 1 is yours" plus the row with your desk lit plus Take it. The cord said nothing the headline does not. About 141 with the strip.

Decided 18 September: Keep.

D3 · Next on the phone line keeps a small cord and gives up its caption

As merged in #382. "You're next for a desk" over the small cord with you at its head. The caption ("The first desk to free is yours") goes. About 157 with the strip, the tightest card there is.

Decided 18 September: Keep.

Decide

D4 · A desk free draws the desk row only, not the cord as well

Not merged; the one change proposed here. When a desk is free the small phone lane draws the cord and the desk row, because the state satisfies both rules at once. It is the double C24 found and shrank rather than removed. Under a floor lane with a rail and a caption that pair is about 158, the tallest quiet card. The desk row says which desks are open; the cord adds nobody is being called, which the headline already says. With the desk row only: about 126.

Decided 18 September: Yes.

Decide

D5 · The strip is the headline and the clock, nothing else

As merged in #377. Under a hot lane the other lane is one line: its headline at 15 pt and its clock. No caption, no picture. It is what keeps every hot card under the line.

Decided 18 September: Keep.

Decide

D6 · The rule for anything new on the card

Proposed as the standing rule: a hot lane carries its words, its buttons and one picture; a quiet lane carries its words and one picture; a strip carries its headline and clock. A new piece has to take the place of one of those, not sit beside it. Every row in sections 3 to 5 is checked against 160 before it is merged, and the numbers are written in the pull request.

Decided 18 September: Yes.
