Rooms transition sheet

Decided by Jorge on 18 September; the decisions are under each item. Every way a salesperson's phone moves between Home, Live Floor and Phone Line, what moves, how long it takes, and what holds still. Read from src/LeadPerformanceCalculator.jsx as merged through #385. Decide once here; after that a fix to a transition is checked against this sheet.

1 · What is on the screen
  • Home and Live Floor are two panes of one page since C29: side by side, a screen wide each, each its own scroll box. The page holds the data; the panes hold the corner and the floor. The corner's lights and its spine are drawn into the pane itself, absolute to it, not fixed to the screen: a fixed thing inside a box the compositor is moving is laid against the screen mid-travel, and on the phone the rail ran to the bottom edge and snapped back (Jorge's recording, 18 September). Since C74 the page is the phone's own sideways scroller, a pane per stop: the phone moves the panes under a thumb, not a script, so a swipe costs no frame. The seam between the panes sits on a whole pixel and each pane bleeds one pixel past its edge under its neighbour, so no hairline of ground shows through mid-travel; the corner's lights fade out over the last 48 px of the pane instead of stopping at its edge (Jorge, 19 September: A1, A2, A3).
  • Phone Line is its own sheet, kept alive once opened.
  • The ground is two canvases fixed behind everything: haze and lights at one pixel per point, dots at the screen's own pixels. Each tab has a palette: Home green, Live Floor dark with teal, Phone Line blue. The ground is painted from a position along the tab list, so between two tabs it is a blend: the leaving room's light fades as the arriving one comes, on the same curve, so the last frame of a travel is the arrived room's own ground (C72; before it the leaving light stayed at full and the ground stepped at the end).
  • The bar at the foot is one control for all three, with a pill that glides to the chosen tab.
  • The curtain is the green sheet with the Sage mark over a room while it loads. It shows on the first open and never again for a room that is already built.
  • The takeover is "You're up": full screen over the stage on either tab, both rooms.

4 · What I found writing this down

The rooms' scroll memory does not work. The code remembers where the floor and the line were scrolled to when you cross, but it reads window.scrollY, and the page is its own scroll box, so it always remembers zero. Each tab's scroll is kept properly since #385; each room's is not. Crossing to Phone Line and back lands at the top.

The ground follows the thumb only on a real swipe, and that finding was wrong. This sheet first said any moving touch during a blend could hold the ground where the finger stopped. On reading the swipe code again: the ground follows a drag only when the rooms themselves are following it, a swipe between Live Floor and Phone Line, and it finishes on the clock the moment the finger lifts. A tap's blend between Home and Live Floor cannot be held by a touch. R4 below is therefore already how it works, and nothing changes for it. What held the floor's ground between the two screenshots is still not known.

5 · Decisions

R1 · Tap between Home and Live Floor: the stage slides, the cards stay still

As it is. The stage comes in from the side on the wipe; the corner's cards do not lift or fade on top of it.

Decided 18 September: Keep. Superseded the same day by R3: the pane travels instead of the stage, and the cards still do not move on top of it.

Decide

R2 · Each room keeps its own scroll, the way each tab now does

Fix the memory so that crossing to Phone Line and back lands where you were, not at the top. Small, and it would go in the next batch.

Decided 18 September: Yes.

Decide

R3 · Home and Live Floor as two sheets (C29)

Then a swipe between them follows the thumb like every other swipe, and the tab slide becomes the same tiling travel the rooms use. It is the biggest single change on this sheet and touches the corner, the floor and the ground.

Decided 18 September: Next batch. Then, on the C29 proposal the same day: two panes on one track inside the floor page (S1), the travel is the corner's arrival (S2), the pills follow the thumb (S3), You're up snaps to the floor (S4). Built in C29; the rows above read as built.

Decide

R4 · The ground on a tap: blend on the clock, never follow a touch

A tap's blend runs on the clock and cannot be held; only a real swipe, one the rooms are following, moves the ground with the thumb. Decided yes, and on a second reading of the code this is already exactly what it does (see section 4), so nothing changes for it.

Decided 18 September: Yes.

Decide

R5 · The standing rule for a new transition

Proposed: every move runs on one clock, wipe; things that travel together land together; a move ends when its animation says so, never on a timer; and each surface keeps its own scroll. A change to any row in section 3 is written on this sheet in the same pull request.

Decided 18 September: Yes.
