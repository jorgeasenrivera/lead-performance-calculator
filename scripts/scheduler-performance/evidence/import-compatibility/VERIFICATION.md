The final scope covers import selections and the off-reason label. Before geometry and screenshots are retained under diagnostics. The applied-day geometry check was added after the initial matrices and caught the off-reason variant.

A separate WebKit pointer call timed out before opening Import, first on month navigation and then on Make Schedule. The retained trace and screenshot show the Make Schedule action completed and the editor was visible, while Playwright's click call had not completed. The exact cause is unresolved. These navigation handlers are unchanged by this increment. Keyboard completion is separate evidence, not a conversion of the pointer failure into a pass. The PR must remain draft and must not be called fully release-verified on this evidence alone.
