Sage is a work tool for car dealerships: the floor rotation, the phone line, and how each salesperson's month is going. You use it because your store does. This page says what it keeps about you, who can see it, and how to have it removed.

What Sage keeps

  • Your account. Your name, email address, whether you are a salesperson or a manager, and which stores you belong to. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider and is never stored in a form anyone can read.
  • Your day on the floor and the phone line. When you joined the line, your status (waiting, with a customer, at lunch), your table, the FlyBys and T.O.s you send and any note you add, and the day's moves.
  • Your numbers. Your store sends Sage the reports its CRM already emails. Sage reads your name and your counts (leads, calls, appointments, shows, sales) and keeps those. The email itself is not kept. Customer names, emails and phone numbers in deal alerts are removed before anything is saved.
  • Whether you were on the lot. Only if your store has drawn its lot and you allow location. Your phone checks its position against the lot and sends one answer: on the lot, off it, or no answer. Where you are is never sent or stored. If you allow location "Always", your iPhone watches the edge of the lot, and when you leave, the phone itself asks whether you are done for the day. Sage learns nothing unless you answer.
  • Not the camera. The floor's QR code is read by your phone's own Camera app, which then opens Sage. Sage never turns the camera on.
  • Notifications. If you allow them, your phone's notification address from Apple or Google, a random ID for the device, and which store and person it belongs to, so Sage can tell you when you are up.
  • When something goes wrong, and how fast it was. The error, the screen it happened on, the app version, your store, your person ID, a random device ID, and your phone's model and screen size. Speed readings carry the same, without the error.
  • On your phone only. Your name for the sign-in screen, your store, your text size, and a copy of the app so it opens without signal. None of it leaves the phone.

Who can see it

  • Your store's managers see your numbers, your status and the day's history.
  • Everyone at your store sees the floor and the phone line: names, who is up, who is with a customer.
  • The store's TV board shows names and this month's numbers, and can be opened without signing in.
  • Nobody outside your store can read or change the floor or the phone line.
  • If your store sets it up, a report that one of your numbers is wrong goes to the store's Slack or Teams channel, with your name, the number and your note.

Who handles it for us

Supabase holds the database and signs you in. Vercel runs the site and records how fast its pages load. Cloudflare receives the store's report emails and hands them to Sage. Apple and Google deliver notifications. The manager's screens and the TV board load fonts from Google Fonts. The screen for drawing the lot loads map pictures from OpenStreetMap, and the spreadsheet export loads its code from SheetJS; each of those sees your device's address when it does. None of them may use your data for anything else.

What Sage does not do

No advertising. Nothing sold or shared for marketing. No tracking you across other apps or websites. No analytics beyond the error and speed readings above.

How long it is kept

  • Error reports: 90 days.
  • Speed readings: 30 days.
  • A device's notification details: 90 days after the device last used Sage.
  • Your account, your floor days and your numbers: for as long as your store uses Sage, because they are the store's history. They are deleted when you ask.

Your choices

  • Turn location or notifications off at any time in your phone's Settings. Sage still works; it asks instead of noticing.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete what Sage holds about you by writing to jorge.rivera@hollerford.com. Your store's managers can also correct your numbers and remove you.

Children

Sage is for people at work. It is not meant for children and does not knowingly keep anything about them.

Changes

If this changes, the date at the top changes with it, and anything that matters is said in the app first.
