Help with Sage

Something wrong on the floor or with your numbers? Tell your manager first; they can fix most of it in a minute. In the app, "Something looks wrong" sends a number back with a note.

Can't sign in? Tap "Forgot?" on the sign-in screen. If your store has not set up your account yet, your manager does that.

Anything else, write to Jorge Rivera at jorge.rivera@hollerford.com. Say which store, and what you were doing.

Delete your account: in the app, You, then Your account, then Delete my account. Privacy: what Sage keeps, at sageonline.io/privacy.
